Humash Native — Support

Help for חומש קולי, the Hebrew Humash reading and audio app.

Need help?

Email shmuel.k89@gmail.com and include your device model, operating system version, and a short description of the problem. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Common questions

Audio will not play. Confirm that the device is not muted, close and reopen the App, and make sure the relevant reading has audio available. Some readings are marked “audio coming soon.”

My playback position or speed changed. Playback position and preferences are stored locally on the device. Deleting the App or clearing its data may remove them.

Zmanim are unavailable. The App needs optional “When In Use” location access to calculate local zmanim. Enable Location Services for the App in device settings, then reopen the zmanim screen. Reading, audio, and calendar features do not require location access.

The Hebrew calendar or date looks different. Check the device date, time zone, and calendar settings. Zmanim also depend on the selected date and device location.

Privacy

Humash Native does not require an account and the current release does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking. Read the full Privacy Policy.
